About

SRM Institute of Science and Technology (SRMIST), commonly known as SRM University, is a premier educational institution in India known for its excellence in teaching and research. Key points about SRM include:

  • Campus Locations: SRM has multiple campuses, with the main campus located in Kattankulathur, Tamil Nadu, and others in Ramapuram, Vadapalani (both in Chennai), and NCR (Delhi).
  • Diverse Programs: It offers a wide range of undergraduate, postgraduate, and doctoral programs across various disciplines, including engineering, medicine, management, science, and humanities.
  • Global Recognition: SRM is recognized for its high standards of education and has achieved notable rankings both nationally and internationally.
  • State-of-the-Art Facilities: The university boasts modern infrastructure with well-equipped labs, libraries, hostels, and sports facilities.
  • Research and Innovation: SRM emphasizes research and innovation, encouraging students and faculty to engage in cutting-edge research projects.
  • Industry Collaboration: The institution has strong ties with industries, facilitating internships, placements, and collaborative research opportunities for students.
  • International Partnerships: SRM has collaborations with numerous foreign universities, enabling student exchange programs and joint research initiatives.
  • Holistic Development: The university focuses on the overall development of students, offering various extracurricular activities, including cultural fests, sports, and technical competitions.
